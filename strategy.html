How we invest

Long-only, fundamentals-first, concentrated in small and mid-cap equities where research effort still buys an edge. AI widens the funnel. It never replaces the thesis.

The process

1. Machine-scale screening

AI agents sweep filings, transcripts, insider activity, and price dislocations across thousands of small caps each week. The output is not a buy list. It is a stack of leads with a stated reason each one might be mispriced.

2. Human underwriting

An analyst takes a lead and tries to kill it. They rebuild the numbers, read the filings themselves, and test the thesis against the strongest bear case they can construct. Most leads die here. That is the point.

3. Adversarial review

Surviving theses go through structured red-team review: independent checks on valuation, balance sheet, dilution, and anything the original work missed. Only theses that survive refutation become memos.

4. The memo

Every position gets a written memo: thesis, business overview, valuation, risks, and catalysts. If we cannot write it clearly, we do not own it.

What we look for

Businesses we understand

If we cannot explain how the company makes money in two sentences, we pass.

Cheap on the numbers

Priced below peers, below its own history, or below a conservative estimate of intrinsic value.

Neglected by the street

Little or no sell-side coverage. Prices drift furthest from value where nobody is looking.

Limited downside

Clean balance sheets and real assets or earnings under the price. Avoiding ruin comes before chasing upside.

Operators who execute

Management with a track record of good capital allocation and skin in the game.

A reason it re-rates

Cheap alone is not a thesis. We want a catalyst: earnings inflection, forced selling ending, a segment the market has not noticed.

Where AI fits

AI does the work that used to make small-cap research uneconomical: reading every filing, tracking every insider transaction, monitoring every earnings call in the coverage universe. Members spend their time where humans still win, which is judging whether a thesis is actually right. Every fact an agent surfaces gets verified before it enters a memo.

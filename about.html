About the team

AI Investment Team is a new addition to the AI Club at Northeastern. We use the tools of the future to study the market in a more efficient way.

Why we exist

Studying the market the old way takes hundreds of hours of reading filings, screening tickers, and checking numbers by hand. AI changes that. We use it to cover more companies, verify more facts, and get to a real opinion faster than a traditional student fund can.

The tools of the future

Every memo on this site starts with AI agents screening the market and ends with a human making the call. That workflow is where investing is headed, and we think the best way to learn it is to run it now, as students, on real research.

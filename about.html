About the team

AI Investment Team is a new branch of the Student Value Fund at Northeastern University, built around one idea: the next generation of investors will run research with AI, so students should learn to do it now.

Who we are

We are Northeastern students who believe fundamental investing and AI tooling belong together. The parent Student Value Fund has spent years proving students can produce institutional-quality research on neglected small caps. This branch takes that same discipline and points modern tooling at it.

What members do

Analysts own names end to end. You build the screen, underwrite the thesis, defend it in red-team review, and write the memo that goes on this site. Along the way you learn accounting, valuation, and how to direct AI agents without trusting them blindly. That last skill is the one employers do not teach.

How we are structured

The team runs in small pods, each led by a senior analyst. Pods meet weekly to pressure-test active theses. Full-team sessions cover process: how we screen, how we verify AI-sourced facts, and post-mortems on calls we got wrong.

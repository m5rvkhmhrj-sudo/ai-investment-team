About the club

AI Investment Club is a new addition to the AINU club at Northeastern. We pair fundamental investing with AI tooling to find mispriced stocks the rest of the market skips.

We use new technology to research the market

Studying the market the old way takes hundreds of hours of reading filings, screening tickers, and checking numbers by hand. We point AI agents at that problem. Our agents sweep filings, transcripts, insider activity, and price dislocations across thousands of small caps every week. Members spend their hours on judgment instead of data collection, which is where the learning actually happens.

We find innovative ways to create alpha

Alpha comes from doing what other investors cannot or will not do. Our edge is coverage: research cost per name near zero lets us look at nano caps, fresh spin-offs, and dual-class oddities no sell-side analyst touches. When nobody else is looking, prices drift from value. That drift is what we hunt.

Discovering mispriced stocks, thoroughly and completely

Thorough means every thesis survives an adversarial review before it becomes a memo. Independent checks attack the valuation math, the balance sheet, and any fact an agent surfaced. Complete means we sweep the whole universe, not just the names on popular screens. Most candidates die in review. The ones that survive go on this site.

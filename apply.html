Join the founding class

We are recruiting the first analyst class for Fall 2026. No finance background required. We care whether you can think, write, and argue with your own conclusions.

Who should apply

Students from any major. Our best analysts tend to share three traits: curiosity about how businesses work, comfort being wrong in front of peers, and the discipline to check what an AI tells them instead of pasting it into a memo.

The process

Written application

Short answers plus one prompt: name a public company you think is mispriced and give us three sentences on why. We read every submission.

Interview

A conversation about your pick. We do not quiz you on formulas. We push on your reasoning and watch how you handle pushback.

Analyst training

Accepted members complete a training sequence covering accounting basics, valuation, our research workflow, and how we use AI tooling with verification built in.

Apply

Email aiinvestmentteam@northeastern.edu with your resume and the mispriced-company prompt above. Applications for Fall 2026 close in September.

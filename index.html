Fundamental research, run at machine scale.

We are a student-run investment research team at Northeastern University. We hunt for mispriced small and mid-cap stocks the way a value fund does, then use AI tooling to cover more ground than a team our size ever could by hand.

Read our research

What we do

Every position starts as a question: why is this stock priced where it is, and what does the market have wrong? Analysts screen hundreds of neglected names each semester, build models, and write full investment memos. AI agents handle the sweep. Humans make the call.

Why it works

Small companies with little analyst coverage are where prices drift furthest from value. Most funds cannot afford to look there. We can, because our research cost per name is close to zero. The bottleneck moves from reading filings to exercising judgment, which is exactly what we want members practicing.

Selected research

Long-only small and mid-cap equities, sourced by machine-scale screening and underwritten by hand. Every memo below survived adversarial review before publication.

Current memos

NYSE: JXN · Initiated July 2026
Jackson Financial: paying 4.6x earnings for a buyback machine
Annuity writer at 4.6x adjusted operating EPS and 0.76x book, returning around 13% of market cap to shareholders in 2026 while GAAP hedge noise keeps screens and analysts away.
NYSE: MBGL · Initiated July 2026
Mobility Global: a 2-day-old spin-off nobody has to own
Forced index selling of the S&P Global auto-data spin leaves CARFAX plus Polk at 11-12x EBITDA versus 14-18x peers, with a 7.4% FCF yield and 2.6x leverage.
NYSE: DOCS · Initiated July 2026
Doximity: the market priced a monopoly network for permanent decline
Dominant physician network at around 9x EV/EBITDA after a pharma ad budget freeze the market mistook for a broken franchise.
NYSE: G · Initiated July 2026
Genpact: the market prices AI as a killer. We think it pays the bills.
Priced at around 7x forward earnings for AI-driven extinction while its AI segment grows 24% and buybacks shrink the share count.
NYSE: MLI · Initiated July 2026
Mueller Industries: a net-cash copper franchise at 15x with a free data center option
Zero-debt copper products leader at 15x trailing earnings while the market pays nothing for its data center cooling and electrical demand exposure.
NYSE: BBW · Initiated July 2026
Build-A-Bear Workshop: buying back stock at the bottom while the market prices a Google panic
Management retired 8.63% of the company at trough prices while an e-commerce hit touching a quarter of revenue left the stock at 6-7x earnings with a 2.9% yield.
NASDAQ: TAYD · Initiated July 2026
Taylor Devices: a seismic-damper monopoly hiding behind zero coverage
Near-monopoly seismic damper maker with record earnings, $41.7M net cash, zero coverage, at ~13x ex-cash after an overdone 21% backlog-driven selloff.
NYSE: GBLI · Initiated July 2026
Global Indemnity: a debt-free insurer at half of hard book value
Debt-free E&S insurer at 0.52x bond-heavy book, 5.6% yield, $273M discretionary capital, with Fox control as the catalyst path.
NYSE: AXR · Initiated July 2026
AMREP: Rio Rancho land at 1960s cost on a debt-free balance sheet
Nano-cap net-net monetizing Rio Rancho land carried at 1960s cost, ~0.94x stated book that badly understates NAV, $50M cash and essentially no debt.
NASDAQ: INMD · Initiated July 2026
InMode: 4x earnings ex-cash for a business the market left for dead
Cash covers 63% of market cap, revenue just stabilized at +5%, and buybacks compound a 4x ex-cash multiple priced for terminal decline.
NYSE: GENC · Initiated July 2026
Gencor Industries: the market pays $60M for an asphalt-plant maker earning $13M
$155M cash, zero debt, ~$215M cap: ex-cash 4-5x earnings for a highway-funded asphalt plant maker with backlog doubled to $60.5M.
NASDAQ: PDEX · Initiated July 2026
Pro-Dex: the surgical screwdriver spec'd into everyone else's FDA filings
Uncovered surgical handpiece OEM with FDA-filing switching costs, 16% growth, and insider buybacks at 19-20x operating earnings.
NYSE: HBB · Initiated July 2026
Hamilton Beach: the #1 blender brand in America priced for zero terminal value
Category-leading small-appliance brand at ~10x earnings with doubling EPS, dividends, buybacks, and a growing HealthBeacon health leg.
NASDAQ: KE · Initiated July 2026
Kimball Electronics: a regulated-market manufacturer priced at book value
Sticky medical and auto-safety EMS at 0.42x sales and 1.04x book after an auto-cycle selloff overshot the durable medical franchise.
NYSE: CNMD · Initiated July 2026
CONMED: 7.7x earnings for surgical franchises the market priced for decline
Durable surgical niches (AirSeal, Buffalo Filter) at 7.7x forward adjusted EPS near a 52-week low, with margins recovering, guidance raised, and dilution overhang removed.
NASDAQ: PLAB · Initiated July 2026
Photronics: a photomask oligopoly priced like scrap after a 36 percent crash
Largest independent photomask maker at ~10.6x trailing earnings with $588M cash and no long-term debt, quoted post a 36 percent overdone Q2 crash.
NASDAQ: CRWS · Initiated July 2026
Crown Crafts: an 11 percent yield the screens still price as a money-loser
Duopoly-like infant soft goods niche at 0.36x sales with an 11 percent covered dividend, mispriced by a stale negative P/E from a non-cash impairment.
NYSE: MD · Initiated July 2026
Pediatrix Medical Group: the NICU staffing leader after the cleanup
National NICU staffing leader at ~9x EV/EBITDA with reaffirmed guidance and a streamlined hospital-based core the market still underprices.
NYSE: CRD.A · Initiated July 2026
Crawford & Company: the claims adjuster nobody screens for at 11x forward earnings
World's largest independent claims adjuster and TPA at ~11x forward earnings, hidden by dual-class family control and a misleading 31x trailing P/E.
NYSE: NC · Initiated July 2026
NACCO Industries: fee-based coal cash flows priced like a dying stub
Fee-per-ton mining contracts with no capital at risk plus growing royalties at 0.83x book, with 80% Q1 EPS growth, dividend raise, and buyback.
NYSE: MOD · Initiated July 2026
Modine Manufacturing: a 110-year-old radiator maker becomes the picks-and-shovels of AI cooling
Data center cooling sales grow 60-80% under a $4B hyperscaler contract, and the Gentherm spin makes Modine a scarce pure-play at ~24x adjusted earnings.

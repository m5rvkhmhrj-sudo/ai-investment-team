Thesis

Modine sells the cooling hardware that keeps AI data centers from melting. Data center sales grew 158 percent year over year in the most recent quarter, and management guides them up another 60 to 80 percent in FY2027. A $4B long-term capacity agreement with a major hyperscale customer, including a $165M upfront payment, locks in demand through 2029.

The company is also mid-transformation. Modine will spin its legacy Performance Technologies vehicle segment into Gentherm through a Reverse Morris Trust, valued around $1B. After the close, targeted for Q4 calendar 2026, Modine becomes a pure-play climate and data center cooling company. We think the market still prices part of this business like the auto supplier it used to be.

This is a growth position, and we say so plainly. At ~24x trailing adjusted EPS with adjusted EBITDA guided up more than 40 percent, we pay a fair multiple for a company compounding into the largest capex cycle in technology. Our verification pass confirmed every material number here against SEC filings and company releases at high confidence.

Business

Modine, founded in 1916 in Racine, Wisconsin, builds thermal management systems. It runs 2 segments. Climate Solutions covers data center cooling, HVAC, and refrigeration. Performance Technologies covers vehicular and industrial thermal products, and it is the segment being divested.

FY2026 (ended March 31, 2026) marked the 4th straight year of record revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted EPS. Net sales reached $3,181.1M, up 23 percent. Adjusted EBITDA hit $471.0M, up 20 percent. Adjusted EPS was $5.02. GAAP EPS of $2.26 looks weak because a non-cash $116.1M pension termination charge depressed it. Q4 alone grew sales 47 percent and adjusted EPS 53 percent. Data center product sales grew 87 percent for the full year. Net debt sits at $362.8M, only 0.8x leverage, and FY2026 free cash flow was $105.4M.

Why the market is wrong

Screens still see a mixed industrial with a scary 102x trailing GAAP P/E. That multiple is an artifact of the one-time pension charge. On adjusted earnings the stock trades near 24x trailing and under 30x forward, while adjusted EBITDA grows more than 40 percent. Growth investors who look through the GAAP noise get a cleaner picture than the headline number suggests.

The bigger misread is the spin. Modine trades today as a blend of a fast data center cooling business and a slow vehicle parts business. The Gentherm deal separates them, values Performance Technologies at 6.8x trailing adjusted EBITDA, hands Modine roughly $210M in cash, and gives shareholders around $790M of Gentherm stock. RemainCo Modine becomes a scarce pure-play on hyperscale cooling. UBS models the data center cooling business quadrupling by FY2029, and some analysts see data center revenue above $2B by FY2028. Consensus rates the stock Strong Buy with an average target near $341, around 48 percent above the current price. The recent pullback from $323 to $231 gives us entry after the froth cooled.

Valuation

At $230.97 the stock trades at roughly 24x FY2026 adjusted EPS of $5.02 and about 29.6x forward earnings. FY2027 guidance calls for total sales up 20 to 35 percent, data center sales up 60 to 80 percent, and adjusted EBITDA of $650M to $680M, growth above 40 percent. Take the guidance midpoint of $665M and the current enterprise value of roughly $12.6B, and the stock trades near 19x forward EV/EBITDA for 40 percent growth. Vertiv and comparable data center infrastructure names carry similar or richer multiples with slower guided growth.

We are honest about the stretch. Some pro-forma analyses put the multiple as high as 35x EV/EBITDA against building-products peers at 22 to 28x earnings. Our position sizing reflects that. If Modine hits the low end of guidance and holds a 20x EV/EBITDA multiple, the stock supports a price near $290. The $341 consensus target requires the high end plus continued hyperscaler orders. We underwrite the low end.

Risks

Valuation is the thesis killer. This price assumes near-flawless execution. Any AI capex digestion period or hyperscaler pause hits the stock hard, and the shares already swing 5 percent or more in a day after a run of roughly 200 percent. We bought a 5.5 percent down day for a reason, and more days like it will come.

Customer concentration is real. A single $4B hyperscale contract carries material weight, and losing or slowing it would gut the growth case. Modine is also a smaller, less entrenched player than Vertiv, Johnson Controls, and Schneider in a capital-intensive, competitive cooling market. Scaling capacity pressures margins. The spin adds execution risk: the deal still needs an S-4, an IRS determination letter, and approvals, and a delay or break leaves Modine holding the legacy segment. GAAP net income fell 34 percent in FY2026, and free cash flow of $105.4M is thin against a $12.2B market cap while capex runs heavy. Tariffs and supply chain costs pressure both segments. Our verification pass flagged the elevated multiple and AI concentration as the main caveats, and we agree they are the whole risk.

Catalysts

Q1 FY2027 earnings arrive July 29, 2026, the first read on the 60 to 80 percent data center growth guide. The Gentherm S-4 filing and IRS determination letter land as mid-2026 milestones, with the spin and merger targeted to close in Q4 calendar 2026. At the close, management updates guidance for the pure-play RemainCo, which forces the market to price the data center business on its own. Data center capacity expansion completes by end of FY2027, March 2027, converting the $165M prepaid commitment into shipped product. Additional hyperscaler order announcements can hit at any time.

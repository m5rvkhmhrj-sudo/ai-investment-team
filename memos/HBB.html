Hamilton Beach: the #1 blender brand in America priced for zero terminal value

Recommendation
Long

Thesis

Hamilton Beach claims the #1 unit-share position in US small kitchen appliances, a category it has led for decades. The market prices the company like a dying appliance maker at a ~$284M cap. Yet Q1 2026 EPS nearly doubled to $0.26 from $0.13, on operating profit up 115 percent, even as revenue dipped 8.6 percent to $122M.

The company generates consistent free cash flow, pays a $0.12 quarterly dividend (~2.3 percent yield), buys back stock, and grows a higher-margin connected-health leg through HealthBeacon. FY2025 produced $606.9M of revenue and $26.5M of net income. Management returned $6.4M in dividends, repurchased 506,925 shares for $9.0M, and authorized a new $25M buyback through 2027.

One correction to our original screen. The thesis pitched 7 to 9x trailing earnings. After a 29 percent run since March 2026, the real multiple is ~10x on TTM EPS of ~$2.08. That is still cheap for a brand with dominant shelf space at Walmart, Amazon, and Target, and well below the 15 to 20x that household products peers have historically commanded.

Business

Hamilton Beach Brands designs and sells small kitchen appliances: blenders, slow cookers, toasters, coffee makers, air fryers. It outsources manufacturing, mostly to China, and wins through brand, distribution, and design speed. The core brands are Hamilton Beach and Proctor Silex, plus licensed lines and a commercial division that sells into restaurants and hotels.

The newest leg is HealthBeacon, a connected-medication-management business acquired out of Ireland. It added $4.3M of sales with subscription revenue growing, and it carries higher margins than the appliance core. It is small today but changes the shape of the business over time. Management reaffirmed 2026 guidance for mid-single-digit revenue growth, net of the Bartesian license expiration.

Why the market is wrong

The market sees a low-growth appliance maker with China exposure and stops there. It misses the margin story. Gross margin hit 29.7 percent in Q1 2026, up 510 basis points year over year, and operating profit more than doubled on lower revenue. This is a company earning more on every dollar of sales while screens still show a shrinking top line.

The market also gives no credit for capital return or for HealthBeacon. At 13.55M shares outstanding, the $25M authorization can retire around 8 percent of the company at current prices. Category leadership in blenders and slow cookers is boring, and boring is why nobody covers this stock. A durable #1 brand with recovering margins, a dividend, and shrinking share count should not trade at 10x earnings.

Valuation

At ~$21 the stock trades at roughly 10x TTM EPS of ~$2.08 and a mid-single-digit multiple of normalized free cash flow. Household products peers such as Spectrum Brands and, historically, Helen of Troy have traded at 15 to 20x. HBB also sits below its own 5-year average multiple despite the recent run.

We do not need peer parity. A re-rate to 13x holds the stock near $27, roughly 29 percent upside, before dividends and buybacks. If HealthBeacon keeps compounding subscription revenue, the market may eventually pay a mixed multiple that values it separately. We treat that as free optionality, not the base case.

One honest adjustment to the earnings run rate. About 190 of the 510 basis points of Q1 margin gain came from selling through pre-tariff inventory, a one-time benefit. Our upside math uses trailing earnings, not an annualized Q1.

Risks

Tariffs are the thesis killer. Hamilton Beach sources heavily from China, so tariff escalation hits gross margin directly, and the pre-tariff inventory cushion has already been spent. Walmart concentration is the second structural risk. The biggest customer holds pricing power over its supplier, which caps how much margin recovery HBB can keep.

Our verification pass flagged 3 more caveats. First, the margin recovery is partly non-recurring, per the 190 basis point one-time benefit above. Second, 2026 revenue faces a headwind from the Bartesian license expiration, which guidance nets against growth elsewhere. Third, the #1 unit-share claim comes from the company itself and lacks independent verification. Our verdict carried medium confidence: no claim was refuted, but the entry multiple is higher than the original pitch and the quarter was flattered.

Catalysts

Q2 2026 earnings in early August will show whether margins hold without the pre-tariff inventory benefit; a clean quarter validates the recovery. Execution against the $25M buyback authorization through 2027 shrinks a 13.55M share float quickly at these prices. HealthBeacon subscription growth compounds each quarter and gives the story a second act. The dividend was recently raised, and further increases would signal management's confidence in cash flow. Any de-escalation in US-China tariff policy removes the main overhang on the multiple.
